# JOURNEY — Olivia Chen

## Scenario

You run a remote B2B consultancy and want a professional Miami presence for stronger proposals and several client meetings each quarter.

## Mission

Find a credible Miami address with clear location evidence and occasional access to meeting rooms.

## Success criteria

- Find at least two plausible Miami locations or plans.
- Understand the area and building information shown.
- Confirm meeting-room availability and disclosed conditions.
- Assess the likely visitor experience from available evidence.
- Select an option or explain why the presentation cannot support a decision.

## Questions

1. Can locations be searched or filtered meaningfully?
2. Are address details and real images available?
3. Is reception availability confirmed?
4. Are meeting rooms included, paid add-ons, usage-based, or not confirmed?
5. Does the displayed price make its included service category clear?

## Entry state

Start at the homepage in a clean session as a visitor searching for a Miami virtual office with meeting rooms.

## Stop condition

Stop before booking, account creation, contact, or purchase.
