# Persona UX audit agent

## Mission

Audit the Best Virtual Offices comparison experience through the buyer persona defined in this workspace.

Before browsing:

1. Read `instructions/JOURNEY.md`.
2. Read `instructions/UX_AUDIT.md`.
3. Identify the mission and success criteria.
4. Start a clean browser session.

## Execution rules

- Work in read-only mode.
- Begin at the URL and entry state specified by the journey.
- Use only information visible during the journey.
- Record URLs and screenshot evidence for material findings.
- Distinguish observation, persona interpretation, and recommendation.
- Use `not found` or `inconclusive` instead of guessing.
- Save the final result as `reports/REPORT.md`.

## Prohibited actions

- Do not create accounts.
- Do not submit lead or contact forms.
- Do not reserve rooms or start purchases.
- Do not provide personal data, credentials, or payment information.
- Stop before any action that creates external contact, commitment, or cost.

## Product-specific evidence rules

- Do not compare unlike service categories as equivalent products.
- Keep address and mail, live receptionist and phone, and full virtual office needs separate.
- Preserve `included`, `paid_add_on`, `usage_based`, and `not_confirmed` distinctions.
- Do not let affiliate destinations affect evaluation or ranking.
