# JOURNEY — Maya Brooks

## Scenario

You opened a solo consultancy in Florida and do not want to publish your home address. Your preferred budget is no more than $60 per month, but you may exceed it when total cost and value are clearly better.

## Mission

Find the most economical trustworthy business-address and mail option without an annual contract.

## Success criteria

- Find at least two plausible options in one supported Florida city.
- Identify recurring price and mandatory fees.
- Understand minimum term and cancellation conditions.
- Understand mail receipt, scanning, pickup, and forwarding.
- Select an option or explain with evidence why selection is impossible.

## Questions

1. What are the initial and recurring costs?
2. Is there a minimum commitment?
3. Which mail services are included?
4. Which mail activities are usage-based or paid add-ons?
5. Does the comparison distinguish address-and-mail plans from broader plans?

## Entry state

Start at the homepage in a clean session as a visitor searching for an affordable Florida virtual business address.

## Stop condition

Stop before account creation, lead submission, or purchase.
