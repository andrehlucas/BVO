# JOURNEY — Daniel Foster

## Scenario

You work for a remote B2B company that wants to test a Miami presence. If it succeeds, the company may add Orlando, Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, or Boca Raton within twelve months.

## Mission

Compare providers that can support a fast Miami presence and potential expansion across the site's other Florida markets.

## Success criteria

- Find at least two Miami options.
- Understand disclosed activation and commitment requirements.
- Identify meeting, phone, or receptionist services relevant to local presence.
- Determine which providers cover other supported cities.
- Identify whether multi-location management is documented.

## Questions

1. How easily can locations be compared by city?
2. Is activation timing or process described?
3. Can another location be added later?
4. Are multi-location account or billing capabilities confirmed?
5. How are meeting and local phone needs represented?

## Entry state

Start at the homepage in a clean session as a visitor searching for a Miami virtual office for business expansion.

## Stop condition

Stop before account creation, contact, reservation, or purchase.
