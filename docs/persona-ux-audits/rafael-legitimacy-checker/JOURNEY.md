# JOURNEY — Rafael Mendes

## Scenario

You live outside the United States and are establishing a Florida company presence. You need a business address with international mail forwarding and clear limits on how it can be used.

## Mission

Find an option suitable for a non-resident founder and determine which needs it solves without confusing it with separate legal services.

## Success criteria

- Find options that appear to accept international customers.
- Identify onboarding documents when disclosed.
- Understand international forwarding when disclosed.
- Distinguish virtual office, business address, and registered agent.
- Select an option or record why suitability remains inconclusive.

## Questions

1. Does the provider serve non-residents?
2. Which address uses are explicitly supported?
3. Which limitations or exceptions are shown?
4. Is registered-agent service included, separate, or not confirmed?
5. Is international forwarding explained?

## Entry state

Start at the homepage in a clean session as a visitor searching for a Florida virtual office for a non-resident founder.

## Stop condition

Stop before account creation, contact, or purchase. Absence of information is not confirmation.
