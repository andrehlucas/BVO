## The quick read

Alliance gives you a clear address-and-mail starting point in Boca Raton, then offers phone, receptionist, coworking, and meeting space around it. The catch is that the public location page does not show a fixed monthly package price or confirm which extras are bundled. You need a written quote before making a fair total-cost comparison.

## What Alliance actually sells

Alliance’s product guide defines its virtual-office core as a recognized business address in your chosen city, onsite mail processing, and access to a place to work or meet clients. Mail forwarding, scanning, lobby listing, live receptionist, virtual phone, coworking, meeting rooms, and private offices sit around that core as add-ons or complementary services.

## What the Boca Raton entry confirms

The recorded Boca Raton location is 4800 N Federal Hwy, Suite B200. Its page lists virtual offices, meeting rooms, live receptionists, virtual phone numbers, and coworking. The product guide supports the address-and-mail model, but the captured location page does not publish a fixed comparable monthly plan price.

Before Alliance or its partners can handle mail, its terms require a completed and notarized USPS Form 1583. Forwarding, scanning, additional names, storage, and related handling can add charges. Where the Boca Raton source does not say an item is included, the comparison keeps it as an add-on or not confirmed.

## Where the total cost gets less clear

Alliance advertises live receptionist, virtual phone, coworking, meeting rooms, and private offices. The reviewed sources do not confirm that those services are included in a particular Boca Raton virtual-office package or show a compatible bundle price. We do not turn separately marketed services into a full-office package on assumption.

No fixed monthly price was captured for the named entry, so the plan requires a quote. Alliance’s terms also allow for security deposits, usage charges, and add-on fees. Ask for the monthly price, deposit, setup costs, mail charges, and any phone or workspace fees on one written breakdown.

## Contract terms to check

Alliance’s terms state a six-month initial virtual-office term followed by month-to-month renewal. Cancellation must be requested through the online portal at least 30 days before the relevant term or renewal. The terms also leave third-party address acceptance for the client to verify.

## What stands out and what to verify

**What stands out:** a specific Boca Raton address, a clearly described mail-processing model, and access to related business services.

**What to verify:** the location-level price, every bundled inclusion, forwarding and scanning fees, and the total first-month cost. This review does not make a claim about service quality or whether another organization will accept the address.
