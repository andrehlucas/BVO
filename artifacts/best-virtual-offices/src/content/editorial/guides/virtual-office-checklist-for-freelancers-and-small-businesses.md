## First, write down the minimum outcome

Write one sentence that describes the job you are hiring the service to do: receive mail, keep your home address private, answer calls, provide a local number, give you meeting space, or combine several of those. That sentence will stop an appealing package name from making the decision for you.

## Before signing, confirm each item

- Confirm the exact street address and whether the listed location is currently available.
- Confirm whether mail is received, how pickup works, and whether forwarding, scanning, postage, or packages cost extra.
- Confirm whether a live person answers calls, included minutes, overages, coverage hours, and routing options.
- Confirm whether meeting rooms, coworking, and private offices are included, discounted, or billed by use.
- Confirm the advertised price, setup fee, promotion end date, minimum term, renewal, cancellation notice, and final billing cycle.
- Complete only the mail-compliance steps the provider requires, such as a CMRA Form 1583 where applicable.

## Do not let the provider answer for a third party

If you intend to use an address for an entity filing, registered agent, bank, license, or platform, verify that question with the organization making the decision. Provider language may describe possible use but is not a substitute for the authority’s rules.
