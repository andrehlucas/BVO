## They solve different problems

Mail handling covers receiving mail and, depending on the plan, notifying you, holding it, allowing pickup, forwarding it, or scanning it. A live receptionist answers calls, takes messages, and may forward calls or schedule appointments. One feature should not be used as evidence that the other is included.

The Opus Florida page lists live call answering and call transferring in its package. Its terms separately say mail handling is associated with an active virtual-office address and that forwarding and service charges are the client’s responsibility. Davinci’s Orlando page lists mail receipt and forwarding at an additional fee while asking buyers to inquire about call answering.

## Questions that make the difference clear

For mail, ask about Form 1583 or other onboarding, pickup, forwarding schedule, postage, scanning, package limits, and what happens after cancellation. For phone service, ask whether a person answers calls, the coverage hours, included minutes, overage rates, greeting and routing choices, and whether a local or toll-free number is supplied.

If a source does not answer a question, label it unknown. A missing included-minute allowance is not proof of unlimited coverage, and an available mail-forwarding option is not proof that forwarding is included.
