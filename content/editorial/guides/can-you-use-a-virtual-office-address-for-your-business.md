## The short answer

Sometimes, but the answer depends on the purpose, the provider’s exact product, the jurisdiction, and the outside organization’s current rules. A virtual-office provider cannot make that decision for a filing agency, bank, licensor, or online platform.

The Florida Department of State’s LLC instructions distinguish a principal-office street address, a mailing address, and a registered agent with a physical Florida street address. That does not decide whether any specific virtual-office plan is accepted for each field. Check the current filing instructions and the provider’s written terms before submitting a form.

## Google Business Profile is a separate question

Google’s Business Profile guidance says businesses need to make in-person contact with customers during stated hours to qualify. Its guidelines say service-area businesses cannot list a virtual office unless that office is staffed during business hours. Whether a specific business is eligible turns on the facts and Google’s current policy, not an address provider’s marketing copy.

## A safe verification sequence

1. Identify the exact use: mailing, public website, entity filing, registered agent, license, bank, or platform listing.
2. Read the relevant authority’s current rules and form instructions.
3. Read the provider’s exact location and plan terms, including restrictions.
4. Get confirmation from the organization that will accept or reject the address when the use is material.
