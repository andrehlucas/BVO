## The number to compare is not always the headline price

Start with the advertised recurring price, then separately identify mandatory setup fees, deposits, recurring mandatory fees, optional add-ons, usage charges, taxes, and any promotion. If a price is quote-only or volume-dependent, do not turn it into an estimate.

The recorded Davinci Orlando location displays a $79 monthly promotion and $50 promotional setup fee, while Davinci’s general FAQ says setup fees start at $150 and promotions can apply. Opus’s Florida page lists $99/month and a $100 setup fee in its own comparison table. Regus’s current terms say a per-location virtual-office activation fee is payable, but do not publish the amount in that document.

## Ask for these terms in writing

- Is the setup fee mandatory, refundable, and per account or per location?
- Does the price change after a promotion, introductory term, or renewal?
- Is forwarding charged by postage, item, shipment, or schedule?
- Are scanning, additional names, storage, calls, meeting rooms, or workspace charged separately?
- What minimum term, renewal, cancellation notice, and final-billing-cycle rules apply?

Usage-based costs are real costs, but not fixed monthly totals. A fair comparison should display them separately instead of pretending they are included or zero.
