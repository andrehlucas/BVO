## Start with the job, not the provider

Do not begin with “Which provider is best?” Begin with “What must this service do for my business?” An address with mail receipt solves a different problem from live phone answering. A plan with both still may not include the workspace you expect.

Use the three comparator tracks: **Address & mail**, **Receptionist & phone**, and **Full virtual office**. The last track has the highest proof threshold because it needs a documented address/mail and phone/receptionist combination that can be purchased together.

## Compare the real cost in two layers

First, compare the recurring base price only where the billing period and included services are alike. Then add mandatory setup fees, deposits, fixed recurring fees, optional add-ons, and usage charges. Promotions should be recorded separately from standard terms.

For example, a source can display a monthly price while leaving mail forwarding, space, or answering overages outside that number. A lower headline price is not automatically lower total cost.

## Check how the service works after you buy

Read the mail setup requirement, pickup and forwarding rules, package limits, receptionist coverage and minutes, workspace booking rules, minimum term, renewal, and cancellation notice. Treat a missing answer as `not_confirmed` rather than `not_available`.

Finally, do not treat the comparison as proof that an address is accepted by a government office, bank, license authority, or platform. Verify the rule with the relevant organization.
