## Product model

Regus publishes separate Business Address, Virtual Office, Virtual Office Plus, and Call Answering categories. Its Miami page says Business Address supports mail receipt and optional forwarding; Virtual Office adds a local phone number and call handling/forwarding; Virtual Office Plus adds five days of private-office or coworking access each month. These statements describe categories, not a promise that every optional item is included in every configured purchase.

## Address, mail, and phone

The recorded Miami entry is 28 W Flagler Street. The provider’s page lists 29 Miami locations and says forwarding incurs additional charges. The catalog treats mail forwarding and scanning as paid add-ons with a quote requirement because the public source does not state a fixed price. It does not infer registration, banking, licensing, Google Business Profile, or registered-agent suitability.

Regus describes call answering, call forwarding, message taking, and local phone service on its Miami page. Because the reviewed source does not establish a comparable included phone/receptionist configuration for the recorded plan, those features are marked `not_confirmed` for ranking rather than converted into points.

## Full-office path and price structure

Regus lists meeting rooms, coworking, private offices, and office-access products in Miami. A Virtual Office Plus plan can include five days of workspace access per month; other workspace access may be on demand or an add-on. The Miami page shows daily starting prices but no fixed comparable monthly total for a single virtual-office configuration, so this catalog keeps the plan quote-required.

The US global terms state that an activation fee is payable for each agreement and that the Virtual Office fee is per location; the amount is not published in the reviewed terms. They also describe monthly advance payment for standard services and variable service charges in arrears.

## Contract observations

Regus says most plans start from one month and asks for 30 days’ notice to cancel on its virtual-office product page. Its Miami page says longer terms can receive discounted pricing. Renewal terms for the cataloged configuration were not captured, so it is not given a numeric rank.

## Verified strengths and limits

The verified strength is a broad set of separately described business-address, call, meeting, coworking, and office options in Miami. The relevant limit is transparency for a fixed like-for-like total: a buyer needs the selected center, package, activation fee, optional forwarding/scanning, and term before comparing it with another offer.
