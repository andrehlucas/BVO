## Product model

Opus’s Florida page presents an all-inclusive virtual-office package at recorded addresses in Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, and Boca Raton. The page lists live call answering, a prestigious business address, company phone/fax number, premium call transferring, and voicemail/fax converted to email.

## Address, mail, and phone

The five captured Florida entries each display $99 per month. The source supports a business address and phone/answering features, but it does not expressly describe included mail receipt in the named package. The catalog therefore does not promote Opus to the address-and-mail or full-office track on an assumption.

The package is relevant to the receptionist-and-phone track because the source explicitly lists live call answering, a company phone/fax number, and call transferring. It does not publish a comparable allowance of included live-answering minutes, so the result carries an insufficient-data label rather than a numerical score.

## Full-office path and price structure

Opus’s page lists a $99 monthly package and its own comparison section displays a three-month contract and $100 setup fee. The terms say setup fees, when applicable, and recurring fees are payable in advance; usage above included communications thresholds can generate charges. Mail forwarding and other requested actions can also be billed at applicable rates.

The reviewed sources do not establish an included workspace or meeting-room package that is mutually combinable with the Florida virtual-office offer. That absence is not presented as unavailable; it remains not confirmed for this catalog entry.

## Contract observations

The current terms state an initial three-month term unless otherwise noted, automatic successive renewal, and a termination request at least 30 days before the end of the term. The service then ends on the last day of the following complete recurring billing cycle. Opus also says its published retail rates may be promotional or changed with 30 days’ notice.

## Verified strengths and limits

The verified strength is a consistently published Florida price and city-specific addresses alongside stated phone-answering features. The key limit is comparison completeness: the package does not publish a standardized receptionist-minute allowance or enough address-and-mail detail to support a full bundle comparison.
