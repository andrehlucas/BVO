## The quick read

Opus offers the clearest headline package in this comparison: $99 per month at recorded locations in five Florida cities, with live call answering, a business address, company phone and fax, call transferring, and voicemail or fax delivered by email. The package is easy to understand at a glance, but two missing details matter: included receptionist minutes and explicit mail-receipt terms for the named Florida offer.

## What the $99 package confirms

The recorded locations are in Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Fort Lauderdale, and Boca Raton. Each displays the same $99 monthly price. The official Florida page supports live call answering, a company phone and fax number, and call transferring.

## Why it is not ranked for every need

The source supports a business address, but it does not expressly say that mail receipt is included in the named package. We therefore do not place Opus in the address-and-mail or full-office ranking by assumption.

The package is relevant to receptionist and phone buyers. However, Opus does not publish a comparable allowance of included live-answering minutes on the reviewed page. Without that number, a $99 plan cannot be fairly compared with a plan that includes a defined call allowance, so it remains unranked.

## Setup fees, usage and workspace

Opus’s page lists a $99 monthly package and its own comparison section displays a three-month contract and $100 setup fee. The terms say setup fees, when applicable, and recurring fees are payable in advance; usage above included communications thresholds can generate charges. Mail forwarding and other requested actions can also be billed at applicable rates.

The reviewed sources do not establish an included workspace or meeting-room package that can be combined with the Florida offer. We keep that information as not confirmed rather than calling it unavailable.

## Contract terms to check

The current terms state an initial three-month term unless otherwise noted, automatic successive renewal, and a termination request at least 30 days before the end of the term. The service then ends on the last day of the following complete recurring billing cycle. Opus also says its published retail rates may be promotional or changed with 30 days’ notice.

## What stands out—and what to verify

**What stands out:** a consistent published price across the five recorded Florida locations and a clearly stated set of phone-answering features.

**What to verify:** included receptionist minutes, overage charges, explicit mail handling, and any workspace option you expect to use. Those gaps prevent a complete like-for-like comparison today.
