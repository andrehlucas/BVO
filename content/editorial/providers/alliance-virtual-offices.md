## Product model

Alliance’s product guide defines a virtual office as a recognized business address in a chosen city with onsite mail processing and a place to work or meet clients. It describes mail forwarding, mail scanning, lobby listing, live receptionist, virtual phone, coworking, and hourly meeting-room or private-office rental as add-ons or complementary services.

## Address, mail, and phone

The recorded Boca Raton location is 4800 N Federal Hwy, Suite B200. Its page lists virtual offices, meeting rooms, live receptionists, virtual phone numbers, and coworking. The product guide supports the address-and-mail model, but the captured location page does not publish a fixed comparable monthly plan price.

The terms say clients need a completed and notarized USPS Form 1583 before Alliance and its partners can handle mail. Forwarding, scanning, handling for additional names, storage, and forwarding may have additional charges. The catalog records these as add-on or not-confirmed features where a location-specific inclusion is not stated.

## Full-office path and price structure

Alliance advertises live receptionist, virtual-phone, coworking, meeting-room, and private-office services, but the reviewed sources do not confirm that those services are included in a particular Boca Raton virtual-office package or provide a compatible bundle price. The catalog does not manufacture a full-office path from separately marketed services.

No fixed monthly price was captured for the named entry, so the plan is quote-required. The terms say security deposits may be required and that certain usage and add-on charges can apply. A buyer should request a written breakdown before comparing first-month and recurring costs.

## Contract observations

Alliance’s terms state a six-month initial Virtual Office term, then month-to-month renewal. A cancellation request must be submitted through the online portal at least 30 days before the relevant term or renewal. The terms also state a client is responsible for checking whether a third party accepts an address.

## Verified strengths and limits

The verified strength is a clearly described address-and-mail model with a specific Boca Raton location and related services. The limitations are missing location-level price and inclusion details, plus variable charges for forwarding and other add-ons. This review makes no claim about third-party address acceptance or service quality.
