## Product model

The recorded Davinci entry is Alafaya Trail Executive Suites at 626 N Alafaya Trail, Suite 206 in Orlando. Its public location page describes a local professional business address, mail receipt, forwarding at an additional fee, client drop-off/pickup, a lobby greeter, and hourly private-day-office and conference-room access.

## Address, mail, and phone

The location page supports a business address, mail and package receipt, local pickup point, and forwarding on request at added cost. It says to ask about call answering, auto receptionist, or web chat, rather than establishing an included phone/receptionist plan. The catalog consequently does not infer phone-track eligibility.

The provider FAQ says a completed and notarized CMRA mail form is required before setup is complete. This is mail-onboarding context only, not advice about registration, licensing, banking, or other external use of an address.

## Full-office path and price structure

The captured location page displayed a promotional $79/month price and $50 one-time setup fee. The catalog keeps the promotion distinct from the recurring price. That page also lists **two hours of conference-room time included**, but does not state the allowance period or a charge for time beyond it. Private-day-office rates vary by location; the FAQ says most locations offer conference room time by the hour.

No included live receptionist or compatible full-office bundle was confirmed for the named plan. Optional space should not be recast as an included full virtual office.

## Contract observations

Davinci’s FAQ says location solutions require a six-month commitment, then roll month to month, and require 30 days’ written notice for cancellation after the term. It also says location-service setup fees start at $150 and sales can discuss promotions; this is why the captured $50 is labeled as a location-page promotion rather than a universal fee.

## Verified strengths and limits

The verified strength is unusually detailed location-level disclosure for the recorded Orlando entry: address, promotion, setup fee, term, mail receipt, pickup, and hourly space. The key limits are promotional pricing and missing confirmation of included phone/receptionist service. This review makes no service-quality or third-party acceptance claim.
