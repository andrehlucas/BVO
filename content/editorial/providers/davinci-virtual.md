## The quick read

Davinci publishes more location-level detail than many providers for its Alafaya Trail entry in Orlando. You can verify the address, mail receipt, pickup, a promotional price and setup fee, and some workspace access. The main gaps are the standard post-promotion price and an included phone or live-receptionist package.

## What the Orlando entry confirms

The recorded location is Alafaya Trail Executive Suites at 626 N Alafaya Trail, Suite 206. Its public page describes a professional business address, mail receipt, client drop-off and pickup, a lobby greeter, and access to private day offices and conference rooms.

## Mail is included; forwarding is extra

The location page supports a business address, mail and package receipt, local pickup point, and forwarding on request at added cost. It says to ask about call answering, auto receptionist, or web chat, rather than establishing an included phone/receptionist plan. The catalog consequently does not infer phone-track eligibility.

Davinci’s FAQ says a completed and notarized CMRA mail form is required before setup is complete. That is a mail-onboarding requirement, not proof that the address will work for registration, licensing, banking, or another outside use.

## The advertised price is promotional

The location page labels $79 per month and a $50 one-time setup fee as a promotion. We show those numbers, but we do not treat $79 as the ongoing monthly price or use it to rank Davinci until a standard recurring rate is verified.

The page also lists **two hours of conference-room time included**, without stating the allowance period or the price beyond it. Private-day-office rates vary by location, and the FAQ says most locations offer conference rooms by the hour.

No included live receptionist or compatible full-office bundle was confirmed for the named plan. Optional space should not be recast as an included full virtual office.

## Contract terms to check

Davinci’s FAQ says location solutions require a six-month commitment, then roll month to month, and require 30 days’ written notice for cancellation after the term. It also says location-service setup fees start at $150 and sales can discuss promotions; this is why the captured $50 is labeled as a location-page promotion rather than a universal fee.

## What stands out—and what to verify

**What stands out:** unusually detailed public information for one Orlando location, including the address, promotion, setup fee, term, mail receipt, pickup, and workspace details.

**What to verify:** the standard price after the promotion, the period attached to the two included conference-room hours, and whether you need a separately priced phone or receptionist service. This review makes no service-quality or third-party acceptance claim.
