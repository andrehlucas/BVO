## What this launch catalog verifies

The catalog records an Opus Virtual Offices entry at 200 E Robinson Street and a Davinci Virtual entry at 626 N Alafaya Trail. Davinci’s page displays a promotional $79/month price and a $50 one-time setup fee for that specific entry; the promotion is not used as a recurring price or in a numeric ranking.

An Orlando address is only one part of the decision. Mail handling, phone coverage, in-person space, required onboarding, and exit terms can matter more than the headline monthly number.

## Choose the right track

- **Address & mail:** Davinci’s recorded Alafaya Trail entry lists a professional address, mail receipt, a client pickup point, and forwarding at an additional fee. Its FAQ states a six-month commitment, then month-to-month renewal, with 30 days’ written cancellation notice after the term.
- **Receptionist & phone:** Opus’s Florida listing shows its $99/month package at the Robinson Street entry, including live call answering and call transferring. Its published allowance is not expressed as a comparable minute count.
- **Full virtual office:** This is a stricter comparison: the address and mail services must be confirmed alongside a compatible live-answering and phone service. The catalog does not infer that a location’s lobby greeter or optional call answering equals an included receptionist plan.

## Limits to keep in view

Davinci’s page says conference-room and private-office rates vary by location. Its location-services FAQ says setup fees can start at $150, while the captured location page shows a particular promotional fee. Verify the live terms before buying. The catalog does not assess whether an address works for registration, banking, licensing, or a Google Business Profile.
