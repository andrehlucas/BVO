## What this launch catalog verifies

The catalog records an Opus Virtual Offices entry at 848 Brickell Avenue and a Regus entry at 28 W Flagler Street. Regus lists multiple Miami virtual-office locations, but this launch dataset records only the address independently captured for the catalog. Check the [source record](/methodology) and a provider’s current checkout before relying on price or availability.

Miami is a useful place to begin only when the address and service package match the work you actually need. A published city listing is not a claim that every provider has the same service, price, or third-party acceptance.

## Choose the right track

- **Address & mail:** Start here when a business address and mail receipt are the core need. Regus’s Miami page describes business-address mail receipt and optional forwarding; its fixed comparable monthly package total was not captured.
- **Receptionist & phone:** Opus publishes a $99/month Florida package at its Miami entry with live call answering, a company phone/fax number, and call transferring. The page does not give a comparable included-minute allowance, so it is shown with a data limitation rather than a numeric rank.
- **Full virtual office:** Use this only where a purchasable address-and-mail component and phone/receptionist component are both verified. This launch catalog does not treat separate products as a bundle unless the provider confirms they can be bought together.

## Limits to keep in view

Regus says forwarding can carry additional charges and prices vary by location and product. Opus’s terms say a virtual-office address is not guaranteed for licensing, maps, directory listings, or financial applications. Those are provider statements, not a substitute for checking the applicable third party’s rules.
