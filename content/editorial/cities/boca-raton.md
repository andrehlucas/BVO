## What this launch catalog verifies

The catalog records an Opus Virtual Offices entry at 1825 NW Corporate Blvd and an Alliance Virtual Offices entry at 4800 N Federal Hwy. Alliance’s location page identifies virtual offices, meeting rooms, live receptionists, virtual phone numbers, and coworking, while its product guide defines the virtual-office core as a city address with onsite mail processing.

Boca Raton has two differently documented product models in this launch set. They should not be treated as interchangeable simply because both use the phrase “virtual office.”

## Choose the right track

- **Address & mail:** Alliance’s product guide supports an address-and-mail model, but the captured Boca Raton source does not publish a comparable monthly package price. Its terms say forwarding and other add-ons can incur charges.
- **Receptionist & phone:** Opus lists its $99/month package at the Corporate Boulevard address with live call answering, a company phone/fax number, and call transferring. The comparable included-minute limit is not confirmed.
- **Full virtual office:** A full-office result requires source evidence that the address/mail and receptionist/phone pieces are bought together. The catalog does not combine separately listed Alliance services without that confirmation.

## Limits to keep in view

Alliance’s terms require a six-month initial virtual-office term, then month-to-month renewal, with a 30-day portal cancellation request. They also state that third parties, including banks and online platforms, can have their own address-acceptance rules. Verify directly with the relevant organization.
