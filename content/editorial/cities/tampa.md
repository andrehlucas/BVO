## What this launch catalog verifies

The launch dataset records an Opus Virtual Offices entry at 401 East Jackson Street, Suite 2340. The provider’s Florida page publishes a $99/month price at that entry and identifies live call answering, a business address, a company phone/fax number, and call transferring.

That is verified coverage, not a claim that this one entry represents every Tampa virtual-office option. More providers may sell services in the area; they should be added only after the relevant location and plan facts are reviewed.

## Choose the right track

- **Address & mail:** A business address alone is not automatically comparable to a package with mail receipt or forwarding. The present Tampa record does not confirm that the published Opus package includes mail receipt, so it is not presented as an address-and-mail ranking result.
- **Receptionist & phone:** The Opus package is the relevant recorded track because its official page lists live call answering, a business phone/fax number, and call transferring. The package’s included answering-minute allowance is not published in a comparable way.
- **Full virtual office:** The record remains incomplete for this stricter track because the catalog has not confirmed an included address-and-mail component with the telephone offering as a single purchasable composition.

## Limits to keep in view

Opus’s current terms set a three-month initial term unless otherwise noted, renew automatically, and require a termination request at least 30 days before term end. Its terms also put forwarding and service charges on the client. Confirm the service selected in checkout, including any mail requirements and fees.
